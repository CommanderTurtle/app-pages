About

Flashpointis a standalone Flash game player using the Ruffle emulator.

Upload yourruffle.jsruntime files and.swfgames to play them in-browser.

Ruffle is an open-source Flash Player emulator written in Rust, compiled to WebAssembly.

No external servers or APIs are used. Everything runs locally in your browser.
